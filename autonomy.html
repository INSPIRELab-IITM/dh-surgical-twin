Autonomy data

The simulator as a source of training data for learning-based surgical autonomy: reinforcement learning from images, imitation learning, vision-language-action models. A policy acts through the master protocol, under the same limits as a human master, on the same SOFA physics.

The environment

import dh                                   # before SOFA, as every entry point
from dh.autonomy import SurgeryEnv
env = SurgeryEnv(task="gallbladder_retract", image_size=(224, 224))
obs, info = env.reset(seed=0, options={"randomise": "light"})
obs, reward, terminated, truncated, info = env.step(env.action_space.sample())
  • The product's simulator in the caller's process: the same Simulation, scene and physics as the portal, with no web server and no page; the Gymnasium API.
  • A policy is one more master. Its action goes through the console as a human master's does; nothing in the environment computes a force or writes a position.
  • A fixed step: by default 2 × 1/60 s physics steps per control step, 30 Hz control at 60 Hz physics, on a simulated clock, so an episode does not depend on how fast the machine runs it.
  • Instability is a failure: a rollback or a reset by the simulator's own guards ends the episode.

Observations and actions

Observations

Images from the laparoscope (a stereo pair optional) and a camera at each instrument's wrist, rendered by the native renderer as a surgeon sees them; a 14-value state (each tip's pose in the laparoscope's frame and its jaw). The forces, the step's language and privileged state are in info.

Actions

Seven values per hand in three modes: increments in the laparoscope's frame (the default), absolute targets, or the master device's own pose. Every step is recorded in all three forms, so a learner picks one and an episode replays exactly.

Tasks, language, randomisation

  • Two tasks are built: reach on the clutch-and-reach board and gallbladder_retract in the cholecystectomy with every abdominal organ live, each with success, failure, reward and a scripted expert.
  • Language on three levels on every step: the task's instruction, the segment being done, and a correction where the expert recovers.
  • Randomisation per episode (camera, light, grasp point and more), recovery episodes from perturbed states, and exact replay.

The dataset

python -m dh.autonomy collect writes the LeRobot v2.1 layout: one video per camera and episode, the state, the grasp forces, the three action forms, reward and success, recovery flags and language annotations, plus a per-episode record of the simulator's setup. An RLDS view of an episode is provided for RLDS-based learners.
